How we plan campaigns

Sample approaches for common types of local business.

Sample approach

Dental clinic

Problem
Few new patients find the clinic online, and most enquiries come only from word of mouth.
What we would do
Google Ads on high-intent searches such as "dentist near me", a tidy Google Business Profile and a WhatsApp booking button.
What we would track
Calls, WhatsApp messages and booked appointments per rupee spent.
Sample approach

Coaching institute

Problem
A new batch is about to start, and parents nearby do not know the institute exists.
What we would do
Instagram and Facebook lead-form ads aimed at parents in the area, plus Reels showing results and faculty.
What we would track
Lead-form submissions, cost per lead and admissions closed.
Sample approach

Local retail store

Problem
Footfall drops outside festival season, and the Instagram page posts only now and then.
What we would do
A monthly content calendar, local SEO for "shop near me" searches and remarketing to past visitors.
What we would track
Store visits, direction requests, calls and follower growth.
Sample approach

Real estate

Problem
A new project or plot layout gets plenty of clicks, but few serious buyers book a site visit.
What we would do
Google Ads on searches such as "flats for sale" and "plots near me", Meta lead-form ads with walkthrough videos, and quick WhatsApp follow-up on every enquiry.
What we would track
Qualified enquiries, site-visit bookings and cost per site visit.

Our working process

Six steps, from understanding your business to improving results every month.

  1. Business understanding

    Your business, customers, location, competition and goals.

  2. Digital audit

    A review of your website, Google presence, social media and current campaigns.

  3. Strategy

    The right channels, audience, keywords, content and campaign objectives.

  4. Execution

    We launch and manage SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads and social media.

  5. Tracking

    Enquiries, calls, messages, traffic, rankings and campaign performance.

  6. Optimization

    Performance data improves targeting, creatives, keywords and conversion paths.

Our approach: business outcomes rather than vanity metrics. Every activity connects to visibility, enquiries, leads and measurable growth.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

It depends on the services and your goals. Ad spend is paid directly to Google or Meta, and our fee is separate. Share your goal with us and we will suggest a plan and a budget.

How soon will I see results?

Paid ads can bring enquiries within days of launch. SEO and social media build over a few months. We tell you what to expect for each channel before we start.

Will I know where my money goes?

Yes. You get regular reports covering spend, enquiries, calls, messages and what we plan to improve next.
